04 / ABOUT

Engineering access. Governing risk.

I work at the intersection of identity engineering, cybersecurity, governance, and automation.

My IAM work focuses on complete identity lifecycles — how access is authenticated, requested, approved, provisioned, reviewed, and removed. My governance work connects those technical controls back to organizational risk, assurance, compliance, and decision-making.

The goal of this portfolio is not simply to show product configuration. It is to document how security systems are designed, why controls exist, how they behave, and what happens when something changes.

Identity Secure access across people, applications, privileges, and workloads.
Automation Reduce manual access administration and enforce repeatable lifecycle decisions.
Governance Ensure access and technology decisions remain justified, reviewable, and accountable.
Risk Connect technical controls to business impact, assurance, and remediation.
