04 / ABOUT

Engineering access. Governing risk.

I work across identity engineering, cybersecurity, governance, and automation.

My identity work focuses on complete access lifecycles — how identities authenticate, request access, receive authorization, move through the organization, undergo review, and ultimately lose access when it is no longer justified.

My GRC and AI governance work evaluates those systems from another perspective: risk, control effectiveness, assurance, third-party exposure, compliance, and responsible technology governance.

Together, the Autumn Solutions projects model how technical engineering and security governance operate inside the same enterprise rather than as separate disciplines.

Identity Secure access across people, applications, privileges, and workloads.
Engineering Automate identity operations and integrate security systems using APIs and repeatable workflows.
Governance Ensure access and technology decisions remain justified, reviewable, and accountable.
Risk Connect technical controls to business impact, assurance, compliance, and remediation.
