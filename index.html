04 / About

Security Engineering With Governance Context

I work across identity engineering, cybersecurity governance, automation, risk, compliance, and AI governance.

My identity work focuses on how access is authenticated, authorized, provisioned, governed, reviewed, and removed throughout the enterprise lifecycle.

My GRC and AI governance work evaluates security through risk, control effectiveness, compliance, assurance, third-party exposure, and responsible technology governance.

The Autumn Solutions portfolio brings these disciplines together inside one enterprise environment. The goal is to show how technical security controls and governance decisions support each other in practice.

Identity Secure workforce, application, privileged, and workload access.
Engineering Build automation and integrations using Microsoft Graph, Node.js, Python, shell, and repeatable workflows.
Governance Keep access and technology decisions controlled, documented, reviewable, and accountable.
Risk Connect controls to business impact, compliance, assurance, and remediation.
