01 / Challenge

Sensitive access should have a lifecycle, not just an approval.

Autumn Solutions needed a governed method for granting temporary Finance access without relying on permanent group membership or manual administrator cleanup. The control needed to capture business justification, separate the requester from the approver, provision access automatically, periodically recertify the assignment, and remove access when it was no longer required.

02 / Design

Microsoft Entra Entitlement Management as the governance layer.

I created a Finance access catalog and access package containing SG-Finance and the LedgerFlow enterprise application. Taylor Brooks could request the package, while Jordan Lee served as the business approver and recurring access reviewer.

03 / Lifecycle

Request to revocation without manual access cleanup.

01

Request

Taylor requested Finance access and supplied the required business-process answer and justification.

02

Approve & Provision

Jordan approved the request. Entitlement Management then provisioned SG-Finance membership and LedgerFlow access automatically.

03

Review & Deny

The scheduled recurring review activated automatically. Jordan determined that the temporary business need had ended and denied continued access.

04

Apply & Revoke

Entra applied the review result, expired the governed assignment, removed Taylor from SG-Finance, and removed LedgerFlow access without a manual administrator removal.

05 / Outcome

A complete governed access lifecycle.

The final implementation demonstrates that sensitive business access can be governed as a lifecycle rather than a one-time administrative action. Business justification was captured, approval responsibility was separated from IAM administration, access was provisioned automatically, continued need was recertified, and a Deny decision ultimately removed both group and application access automatically.

Key control outcome

No manual removal from SG-Finance or LedgerFlow was used to complete the revocation. The access review decision drove the governance lifecycle through expiration and downstream removal.
